# Scoring rubric retired

The previous weighted CRO/UX/SEO/performance/security/GEO score was retired in version `2026.09.02` because unrelated static proxies could create a precise-looking result unsupported by conversion, ranking or citation data.

Use `prioritization-rubric.md` and `evidence-model.md` instead. A presentation dashboard may summarize finding counts and coverage, but must not calculate an overall quality or visibility grade.
